About

Who We Are

OpenFit is a company located in Natal/RN, specialized in the production and delivery of fitness meals. Our goal is to meet the growing demand for practical, healthy, and balanced meals, especially for those who want to maintain quality nutrition but don't have time to cook. OpenFit offers personalized meal options, catering to the preferences of omnivores, vegetarians, and vegans.

Mission

To provide practical, convenient, and sustainable fitness meal options, helping our clients maintain a balanced diet and achieve their health goals.

Vision

To be a relevant brand in healthy food manufacturing, valuing the health and well-being of our consumers.

Values

  • Transparency
  • Care
  • Quality
  • Innovation
  • Trust

Business Opportunity

With increased awareness about health and wellness, as well as the popularity of remote work and home workouts, the demand for healthy and practical food options has grown significantly. OpenFit differentiates itself by offering personalized meals that meet each client's specific nutritional needs, thus expanding its consumer base.
