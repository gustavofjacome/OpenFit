Work With Us

Explore available opportunities and come be part of our team dedicated to promoting health and wellness. Join us at OpenFit and help transform lives through healthy eating.

Available Positions

Kitchen Assistant

  • Activities: Responsible for performing food preparation tasks, as guided by the cook, ensuring the hygiene of the workplace, receiving and storing food supplies.
  • Education: Minimum – High School.
  • Training/Knowledge: Mastery of culinary processes.
  • Experience: Minimum 6 months.
  • Skills: Attention, ability to work under pressure, skill with various kitchen tools (especially knives), good memory, sense of hygiene, ability to multitask simultaneously.

Kitchen Helper

  • Activities: Responsible for pre-preparation, hygiene, organization, and small food productions in various kitchen sectors. Assist in the kitchen, washing, peeling, cutting, and grating food under the cook's guidance.
  • Education: Minimum – High School.
  • Training/Knowledge: Basic culinary knowledge.
  • Experience: Not required.
  • Skills: Responsibility, organization, care, skill in handling knives and other sharp objects, attention, and method.

Attendant

  • Activities: Customer service, reception, presenting the menu, organizing shelves, stocking, and organizing the premises. Receiving goods and clarifying possible doubts. Ensuring good service, efficiency, and productivity.
  • Education: Minimum – High School.
  • Training/Knowledge: Negotiation and persuasion skills.
  • Experience: Minimum 6 months.
  • Skills: Good communication, flexibility, resilience, problem-solving ability, empathy, emotional control, positive and optimistic attitude, work ethics.

Delivery Driver

  • Activities: Prepare loads, unload goods, and deliver orders to customers.
  • Education: Minimum – High School.
  • Training/Knowledge: Type A driver's license.
  • Experience: Minimum 12 months.
  • Skills: Driving skills, organization, cordiality, punctuality, proactivity, and responsibility.
